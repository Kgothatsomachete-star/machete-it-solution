MEET THE FOUNDER

Built with curiosity. Driven by solutions.

Machete IT Solution was founded by Machete Kgothatso Daniel, who comes from Vuwani in Limpopo. His interest in technology and problem-solving inspired him to build a business focused on practical IT support and useful digital solutions for individuals, students and businesses.

Kgothatso studied Information Technology at Boston City Campus and is continuing his studies as a final-year Diploma in Systems Development student. He is developing his skills in software development, web technologies and systems analysis while building solutions to real-world problems.
